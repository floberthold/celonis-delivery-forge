# User Guide

## Onboarding

1. Sign in at `/login`.
2. Confirm your user appears in `/people-ui`.
3. Ensure your project has at least two active members for 4-eyes governance.

## Daily Workspaces

- `/dashboard`: Central action hub
- `/orchestration-ui`: Quest and assignment management
- `/todos-ui`: Execution tracking
- `/files-ui`: Delivery artifacts
- `/kpis-ui`: KPI lifecycle and formula management
- `/reviews-ui`: Submission and decision flow
- `/timeline-ui`: Audit traceability
