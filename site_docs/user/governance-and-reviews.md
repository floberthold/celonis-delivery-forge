# Governance and Reviews

Delivery Forge enforces a governed workflow where review decisions are explicit and traceable.

## Review Workflow

1. Prepare asset/project change.
2. Open `/reviews-ui`.
3. Submit for review.
4. Assigned reviewer evaluates and records decision.
5. Resolve requested changes or proceed after approval.
6. Confirm timeline entry exists in `/timeline-ui`.

## Core Governance Rules

- Author and reviewer should be different users.
- Project staffing must support dual control.
- Review decisions should be captured before final closeout.

## Practical Checklist for Teams

- Minimum two active members on governed projects.
- Every critical asset change has a review event.
- Requested changes include a clear correction path.
- Final approvals are visible in history.

## Common Errors

- Cannot submit review: missing permissions or project membership context.
- Cannot close project: unresolved reviews or state constraints.
- Reviewer cannot see request: org/project scope mismatch.
