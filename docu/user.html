1) Onboarding Checklist

  1. Open Dashboard and verify you can view clients, projects, assets, and reviews.
  2. Confirm your account appears on People with the expected global role.
  3. Join the target project via membership assignment before creating delivery artifacts.
  4. Confirm at least two active members are assigned to support 4-eyes approvals.

2) Delivery Workflow

Use this flow as the default path for consulting delivery work from planning to closure.

3) Governance Rules You Must Respect

Rule What It Means in Daily Work
Author and reviewer must differ Never assign yourself as reviewer for your own change request.
At least two active project memberships Projects without dual control cannot be moved to active.
Only assigned members can submit/review Ensure asset and project membership coverage before submission.
Timeline audit entries are mandatory Track events in Timeline for traceability and client governance checks.

4) Recommended Role Split

Author

Creates or updates assets and submits changes for review with clear summary notes.

Reviewer

Validates quality and governance; decides approve or request changes.

Delivery Lead

Maintains membership setup, release readiness, and timeline quality.

6) Troubleshooting

If a submit or decision fails, first check membership assignment and role split (author ≠ reviewer).
  • Missing project in selection lists: create client first, then project.
  • Review not allowed: verify two active project memberships.
  • Asset update blocked by process: inspect current review status and timeline events.
  • Celonis action fails: verify tenant connection card and token settings on Dashboard.
