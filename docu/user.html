Open Guide

1) Onboarding Checklist

  1. Open Login and authenticate with your local account credentials.
  2. If you are not a member of any organization yet, ask an owner or admin to grant access before continuing.
  3. After login, the app redirects you to your requested page or back to Dashboard.
  4. Confirm your account appears on People with the expected role and project membership coverage.
  5. Confirm at least two active members are assigned to support 4-eyes approvals.

2) Dashboard Action Center

Create From Dashboard

  • Create clients, people, projects, memberships, assets, and review requests directly from dashboard cards.
  • Use dashboard forms when you need a fast setup path without switching between topic pages.
  • Review success and error banners after each action; they usually indicate validation or governance gaps.

Celonis Companion Actions

  • Configure a tenant connection for a client.
  • Run preflight checks before delivery operations.
  • Trigger extract and import actions to test or execute integration calls.

5) Snapshot and Knowledge Tools

Snapshots

Use snapshot pages to capture client state, inspect snapshot details, and download exports, deltas, or replay plans.

Snapshot routes are client-specific and usually opened from client-centered workflows.

KPI Book

KPI Book stores reusable formulas, task templates, and client-specific KPI references for repeatable work.

Treat it as a knowledge base for metrics design and reuse.

6) Governance Rules You Must Respect

Rule What It Means in Daily Work
Author and reviewer must differ Never assign yourself as reviewer for your own change request.
At least two active project memberships Projects without dual control cannot be moved to active.
Only assigned members can submit/review Ensure asset and project membership coverage before submission.
Timeline audit entries are mandatory Track events in Timeline for traceability and client governance checks.
Organization access controls visibility Most pages and actions are scoped to your active organization, so missing data often means missing org membership rather than missing records.

7) Recommended Role Split

Author

Creates or updates assets and submits changes for review with clear summary notes.

Reviewer

Validates quality and governance; decides approve or request changes.

Delivery Lead

Maintains membership setup, release readiness, and timeline quality.

9) Troubleshooting

If a submit or decision fails, first check membership assignment and role split (author ≠ reviewer).
  • Browser redirects to login: your session expired or the requested page requires authentication.
  • Login succeeds but no data appears: verify that your account belongs to the active organization.
  • Missing project in selection lists: create client first, then project.
  • Review not allowed: verify two active project memberships.
  • Asset update blocked by process: inspect current review status and timeline events.
  • Celonis action fails: verify tenant connection card and token settings on Dashboard.
  • Snapshot or KPI Book pages are unavailable: start from the relevant client flow and confirm client-specific access.
