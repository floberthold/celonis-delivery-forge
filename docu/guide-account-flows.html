Account Flows Guide (Step-by-Step)

Detailed operational guide for registration, verification, login, profile updates, and password reset.

1) Register a New Account

  1. Open /register.
  2. Enter full name, email, password, and password confirmation.
  3. Submit form and confirm success message appears.

2) Verify Email

  1. Open your mailbox for the registration email.
  2. Click the verification link.
  3. Confirm redirect to login with activation success banner.

3) Sign In

  1. Open /login.
  2. Enter email/password and submit.
  3. Confirm redirect to Dashboard or preserved target path.

4) Update My Account

  1. Open /account-ui.
  2. Update name and/or email.
  3. Optionally provide new password and confirmation.
  4. Save changes and confirm success banner.

5) Forgot Password

  1. Open /forgot-password.
  2. Submit your email.
  3. Open reset email and follow reset link.
  4. Set new password and confirm login works.

Troubleshooting

  • No verification/reset email: ask admin to verify SMTP variables and sender policy.
  • Reset link invalid/expired: request a new link from Forgot Password.
  • Login succeeds but data is missing: verify your organization membership.
